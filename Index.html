HSRIS is decision-support intelligence, not a lending decision and not a regulated credit-bureau score; it complements your credit policy, KYC/AML and security requirements. Barbados deployment: member data is held and processed under the Data Protection Act, 2019-29; the credit union sector is supervised by the Financial Services Commission under the Co-operative Societies Act. Scores are deterministic, versioned and traceable to evidence; capital-facing steps pass human maker–checker review.
